## UI

### Sidebar Navigation

Main Pages:

1. Home (view all portfolios: personal, ongoing, and ended)
2. Discover (view today's top performing stocks and find stocks by sector)
3. Solo (your solo portfolio)

Leagues:

4. Create (button to create a league)
5. Join (button to join a league)
6. List of leagues (shows currently active leagues)
7. Ended Leagues (click to show the list of leagues that have ended)

Bottom:

8. Profile

---

### Header

Includes page title on the left. On the right is:

- the Search bar (main place to search for stocks)
- notifications button(to open notifications panel)
- chatbot button (to open the most recent conversation)

---

### Floating "New Chat" Button

Button that is always present at the bottom right of the screen that allows you to quickly start a new chat

---

## Actions

### Profile:

#### Sign Out

Navigate to the profile page and find the sign out button on the top right of the page.

#### Change password

Navigate to the profile page and find the "Send Rest Link" button to be sent a password reset email

#### See Your Badges

Navigate your proflile, then navigate to the "Badges" page

#### Change or Delete Your Profile Picture

Navigate to your profile, then hover on your profile picture and click the pencil or trash icon

### Chatbot:

#### Let the Chatbot See My Portfolios

Click the "Add Context" button above the chatbot input to choose up to 6 portfolios to send to AI

#### See Chatbot History

Click the "History" button at the top of the chatbot panel
